Both profiles use OpenGL, 48 kHz stereo and NV12 / Rec. 709 / Limited range, with the main stream encoder reused for recording.

My laptop streams

  • Restream: main OBS output, Apple H.264 at 15,000 kbps.
  • Twitch: Aitum output at 5,000 kbps.
  • Kick: Aitum output at 8,000 kbps.

The Aitum outputs use Apple H.264 with 2-second keyframes and AAC at 144 kbps; the fork aligns the extra canvas to 1672 × 1080 at the encoder to prevent distorted frames.

Recovery controls

Screen capture restarts only when OBS marks it restartable, camera-service restart needs administrator permission and can briefly interrupt cameras, and a full restart uses the separate OBScene app ↗.

Build and setup guide ↗

Both forks are source only, with no published installers; the guide explains the changes, build steps and how to configure your own accounts.
